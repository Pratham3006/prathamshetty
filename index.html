About / Why I build this way

Software that's fast, accessible and built from scratch.

As a software engineer, I construct web interfaces and design systems with a special love for accessibility and performance. I tend to code things from scratch and enjoy bringing ideas to life.

  1. R.01

    Accessible by default. If it doesn't work for everyone, on every device and input, it isn't finished.

  2. R.02

    Performance is a feature. Fast pages are kind pages. Every kilobyte has to earn its place.

  3. R.03

    From scratch, on purpose. I'd rather understand every line than inherit a pile I can't explain.

  4. R.04

    Learn in public. What I figure out, I write down. The notes above are how I keep that promise.

In my free time, I enjoy playing football ⚽
